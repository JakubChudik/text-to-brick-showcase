# Amon Sûl (Weathertop): design brief

## The request

Amon Sûl, the ruined watchtower on its bare hill, rebuilt with the engine's newer capabilities. On the version it replaces, the owner said (21 October 2026): "X seems much better but some sub-scenes like the fireplace need more attention to small details". On the place itself (21 October 2026): "Weathertop: darkness, ill power, uncanny uncommunicable danger, death lurking in the shadow".

## The moment

The Nazgûl attack on the summit at night. The hobbits' fire in the dell below has been seen; five black riders climb the hill; on the ring of broken stones the Witch-king raises his blade over Frodo while Aragorn comes up with a burning torch.

## The feeling

Night and dread: darkness, ill power, danger that cannot be named, death lurking in the shadow.

## Must-sees

- The Witch-king over Frodo, and Aragorn with the torch; the Witch-king lifted visibly above the others.
- A summit that does not read as one grey mass: a darker hill body with lighter ring courses and steps.
- The Nazgûl in real hoods or cowls if a real part exists; otherwise hats.
- The dead tree reading as a bare tree, not a brown block; a larger crag; no dark opening left in the front face.
- The road with visible texture, a larger waymark and dead tree, and one figure on the road (for example a ranger or a hobbit) so that something happens there.
- At the camp fire, the pan where it can be seen, not hidden behind the flames.
- Real minifigures only.

## Size

The plan called for about 40 cm tall (around 125 plates) and around 6,500 parts, on a cored hill; the stated part range is 5,000 to 7,000. The summit should come down towards its planned share of about 1,900 parts (at most about 2,600) by thinning the hidden mass of the hill crown, not the fight. The previous version: 4,847 parts, 48 x 51 studs, 85 plates high, no minifigures.

## What this version had to keep and add

The scene is planned as one big scene broken into four smaller scenes, each built and looked at on its own before assembly. The new version had to keep the night "dread" moment and palette and keep what the previous version won with, then face it in a blind side-by-side pick.
