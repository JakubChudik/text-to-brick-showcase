# The Colosseum: design brief

## The request

The Flavian Amphitheatre in modern Rome, with the Arch of Constantine, as a display landmark diorama seen from the south-west. The owner, 25 October 2026: "build a new colosseum with all the improvements in mind (more parts, more roundness, color unity and differentiation) ... I want to have clear steps and arches and a pit with a fighting scene and then from the back of the colloseum we can have some columns missing and show an armory with real weapons and anvil and weapon rack etc (see how lego rivendell builds these mini scenes) ... and maybe another mini scene with real lego animals in cages etc"

## The moment

The monument as it stands today, in evening light, with modern Rome busy at its feet.

## The feeling

Ancient grandeur in a living city: warm weathered travertine, two thousand years of scars, awe meeting everyday life.

## Must-sees

- The elliptical Colosseum: the full four-storey wall on the north (round arcades, half-columns, closed attic); on the south the outer ring lost, showing the broken second ring.
- The two sloping brick buttresses clamping the cut ends of the outer wall.
- Inside, through the collapse: wooden arena floor, hypogeum, ruined tiers.
- The Arch of Constantine: triple arch, columns with statues, tall attic.
- The city: cobbled piazza, tarmac road with a bus or Vespa, umbrella pines, a fence, tourists.
- More parts and roundness; one travertine family with each structure in its own tone and part types; darker shades only on lines.
- Clear steps, real arches, a gladiator fight in the pit, an opened back with an armory (weapon rack, anvil, forge), caged animals under the stands.

## Nice to have

Missing blocks and putlog holes, grass on the ruined tops, the metro entrance, a souvenir stand, a gladiator re-enactor.

## Size

First brief about 3,000 to 5,000 parts; the second about 4,000 to 6,000. Earlier microfig spectators were about 3.5 times over scale, so a real fight needs a bigger model or microfigures.

## What this version had to keep and add

The stands are judged in the assembled view the previous version won from; if mottled there, their colour is calmed. The botticella is accepted without a horse. Cheap fixes: a tourist out from behind the Arch, detail on the temple podium, a bus that reads as a bus, tourists facing the wall, a taxi that is less of a white slab.
