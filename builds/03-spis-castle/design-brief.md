# Spiš Castle: design brief

## The request

The owner chose Spišský hrad in Slovakia as a landmark for the public showcase (19 October 2026), to be built to a "would post this" bar. No existing LEGO model of it was found, so a good model would be the first.

## The moment

The castle as it stands today: a ruin since a fire in 1780, partly restored, draped along a pale travertine ridge. It is seen from the south or south-west fields, which show the whole ridge from end to end; the display face is to the south.

## Must-sees

1. The pale travertine hill with a band of cliffs under the walls (east and north), laid as horizontal pale layers, not jagged rock. It sets the whole silhouette.
2. The upper castle on the eastern summit: the round keep and the palace block, the one tall vertical.
3. The long chain of curtain walls stepping down the ridge, three enclosures growing larger westward. This is what makes it Spiš and not "a castle"; one compact enclosure fails.
4. Square gate towers along the walls, the lower bailey's central one the biggest.

## Nice to have

The semicircular barbican at the west gate; the small round bastion at the south-west corner; window openings, crenellations and buttresses; red-brown roofs on the restored parts only, the one warm accent; a few dark green trees and village roofs at the foot. Broken wall tops on the lower bailey, while the upper castle stays crisp.

## Colours

Cliffs in mixed tan and light grey with a little white on the brightest faces; walls in light grey and tan, dark tan where weathered; dry-grass slopes in olive green with sand green and green accents; dark green trees.

## Size

Minifigure scale is impossible here, so Spiš is a micro-scale landmark, about 1:520. The brief proposed 96 x 48 studs (77 x 38 cm, about 18 cm high, 5,000 to 7,000 parts); the maximum build area brings this to 64 x 44 studs. Length to width about 3 to 1; the keep is the only tall element above long, low walls.

## What this version had to keep and add

Bring the castle up to the current engine (real minifigures, layered firs, textures and other newer recipes) while losing nothing that won before: keep everything the owner praised, make room for it rather than drop it, and add rather than replace any density the owner liked.

*The full working instructions given to the design agent (tooling, checks and process) are kept in the private engine repository.*
