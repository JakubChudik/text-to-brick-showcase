# Orthanc and the flooding of Isengard: design brief

## The request

The owner (27 October 2026): "a large build of the Orthanc. I want the scene depicted halfway through the battle with the ents --- river flowing, ents with stones in hand, the two hobbits close to a food storage room, fiery pits, orcs and Saruman at the top of the tower. Make sure to fully appreciate the feel and structure and sharpness of the tower Orthanc."

## The moment

Halfway through the Ents' attack: the river Isen bursts through the broken ring wall and floods the plain while Saruman stands at the top of his untouched tower.

## The feeling

The unbreakable black tower, cold, sharp and absolute, standing untouched while the industry at its foot is torn apart by the Ents and the flood: cold dread, awe, and the satisfaction of watching that power's industry drown.

## Must-sees

1. Orthanc, the hero: jet-black, glassy, many-sided, by far the tallest thing. Four keel-sharp piers fuse into the shaft and split into four horn-like pinnacles near the summit; a narrow stair to the door; Saruman in white between the horns. Knife-edged ridges, no studs on the faces, a slender tapering silhouette, shaped rather than built.
2. The flood bursting through the ring wall, across the plain and into the pits.
3. Brick-built Ents, much taller than minifigures, raising stones; one tearing at the wall. They stand beside the wall gaps, not in them.
4. Fiery pits with machinery and chimneys: warm orange fire against cold blue water.
5. Orcs fleeing or fighting, staggered in depth.
6. Two hobbits near a food storage room with barrels and crates, calm amid the chaos.

Works against it: studs, bands or windows on the tower, a squat shaft, grey mottling, a tidy plain.

## Nice to have

Rubble at the breach; steam where water meets fire; Saruman's staff on the polished summit; smoke-blackened and felled chimneys.

## Size

Large: up to 96 studs across and 160 plates high, about 5,000 to 8,000 parts, the tower near the height cap. Minifigure scale for the figures, the tower compressed in height. Hero view from the south-west, low so the tower soars. No copying of LEGO set 10237.

## What this version had to keep and add

Keep the ring wall that won before ("the wall behind Orthanc"), light grey specks included. Ground round the Ents' feet dark, not a grey studded field. Round tree stumps, and a clearer wall behind the tower from the hero view.
