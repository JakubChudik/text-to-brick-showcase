# Ministry of Magic, the Atrium duel: design brief

## The request

The owner (27 October 2026): "a scene from Harry Potter and the Ministry of Magic mid fight between Voldemort and Dumbledore. There was a water fountain in the center with some stone statue, upper and lower gallery of offices, the teleportation hearths, and Dumbledore, Voldemort, Harry and Bellatrix Lestrange, and spells flying, and shards of glass on the floor."

## The moment

The Ministry of Magic Atrium during the duel between Dumbledore and Voldemort (book five, Order of the Phoenix, chapter 36, and the 2007 film).

## The feeling

A grand, cold, polished hall of dark wood and green-black glossy stone suddenly torn by a duel of titans: awe, menace, flying light.

## Must-sees

1. The atrium hall: a polished dark floor, dark wood panelling, a peacock-blue ceiling, and two galleries of offices (lower and upper) with balustrades, windows and doors.
2. The Fountain of Magical Brethren in the centre: a round pool of water with golden statues of a wizard, a witch, a centaur, a goblin and a house-elf, some animated or broken by the duel (one shattered).
3. The Floo fireplaces in a row along a wall, some with green flames.
4. The duel: Dumbledore and Voldemort facing each other across the fountain, spells flying (a green killing curse from Voldemort; a shield or fire from Dumbledore), a water shield or fire whip if possible; Harry behind a statue; Bellatrix pinned or watching.
5. Shards of glass on the floor, and broken gallery windows above.

## Nice to have

Gold symbols on the ceiling; the golden gates at the end of the hall; the headless wizard statue shielding Harry (book); a water sphere around Voldemort (book).

## Size

A large display diorama: footprint up to 96 studs, height up to 160 plates, about 4,000 to 7,000 parts. An open-front cut-away, seen from the front and slightly above (about 20 degrees), looking into the hall. Posed real minifigures. No copying of any official LEGO Harry Potter set.

## What this version had to keep and add

Bring the build up to the current engine (real minifigures, flames, statues, textures and other newer recipes) and lose nothing that won before, in the owner's words: "the Dobby statue is definitely better", plus the bays, the lift, the duel on the pool and the film-based fountain statues.
