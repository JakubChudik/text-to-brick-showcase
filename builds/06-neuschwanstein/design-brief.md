# Neuschwanstein Castle, Bavaria: design brief

## The request

The owner chose Schloss Neuschwanstein as a landmark for the public showcase (19 October 2026). On earlier versions (27 October 2026): "both are great, X is better because the castle is not as flat as in Y, although I like the windows and the extra attached tower of Y, so maybe a combination of the two... also the bridge over the river on X is awesome". And (28 October 2026): "I like the bottom Y with the little birds on the lake and more forests, maybe some trees. That looks amazing. We should definitely be moving in that direction with our build."

## The moment

A day in King Ludwig's woods, seen from the Marienbrücke side (south-west): the king on a Palas balcony, tourists photographing the gorge, masons on the gatehouse, swans on the Alpsee, a hunter and a deer, supplies coming up the road.

## The feeling

A fairy-tale castle on a crag: white limestone towers rising out of a dark forest above a gorge, romantic and vertical, in a living landscape.

## Must-sees

1. The tall white Palas: five storeys, two steep dark slate roofs, recessed arched windows, the attached oriel tower and staggered blocks, never flat.
2. The slender north stair tower with a tall pointed cone, the tallest point.
3. A long, narrow castle on the ridge: turrets of different heights, the square tower, the Bower and the Knights' House round the upper court.
4. The red-brick gatehouse: crow-stepped gable, two stair turrets.
5. The grey crag falling into a forest on several levels: trees of many heights and kinds, undergrowth, clearings.
6. The Marienbrücke over the gorge, its river below.

## Nice to have

Gold finials, pinnacles on the cornice, a forester's camp with a rabbit at the back, a hiker and his dog on the trail.

## Size

A display diorama of about 80 x 40 studs and 5,000 to 7,000 parts in the plan; the castle in micro-scale (about 1:235), the figures real minifigures. No copying of LEGO set 21063: show the crag, the forest and the gorge.

## What this version had to keep and add

Keep everything that won: "the little birds on the lake", the forest, the bridge with its tourists, the Palas "not as flat" with "the windows and the extra attached tower", the vertical skyline, the masons, the life in the landscape, real minifigures. Add a layered fir in the east clearing, light shallows round the lake, a sand road, two gate guards, two travellers and a planted bastion garden.
