# Yoggi the Samoyed: design brief

## The request

The owner chose Yoggi, the owner's young Samoyed, for the public showcase (19 October 2026). On the coat (21 October 2026): "the fur is better but looks more jagged, that's not the essence of a samoyed coat. it should be fluffy, royal, with long white strands that sometimes stick out to the side but sometimes elegantly fall one over another, high polish". On the version that won (28 October 2026): "Y def much better, I like the floor desing and the playful face of the dog and the 3D tail ... it would be great to add more details on legs and paws too".

## The moment

Yoggi sits on a small lawn, waiting beside his red ball among spring flowers.

## The feeling

Royalty, fluffiness, playfulness, smiling beauty.

## Must-sees

1. The playful face: wedge head, tapering muzzle, black nose, dark almond eyes set wide, and the "Samoyed smile" with a pink tongue as the focal point.
2. The ruff, a soft mane wider than the head.
3. The tail: a white plume curled over the back and draped to one side.
4. Small, thick, triangular ears with rounded tips, set wide; not tall and pointed like a Husky's.
5. An all-white coat with no mask, its strands falling in layers one over another, tufts sticking out only at the edges (ruff, cheeks, tail).
6. Four legs and paws with real detail.

Works against it: jagged spikes sticking out everywhere, grey specks that read as dirt, bare studs on the coat, a smooth box that reads as a polar bear or a sheep.

## Nice to have

A hint of cream on the ears, a silver-white sheen, the head slightly turned.

## Size

A pet portrait sculpture: about 44 studs on the longest side, about 30 cm tall with the head up, about 2,000 to 3,000 parts in the plan. At minifigure scale Yoggi would be 1.5 cm tall. Modelled from the breed standard and the owner's own photos, which stay private.

## What this version had to keep and add

Keep the face, the tail, the floor and the body of the version that won, and its paws, which beat a later rebuild ("Y has better paws"). Add what the owner asked for: "a little color to them to further highlight the individual fingers", and more detail on the legs: a tapered forearm, fur locks, the elbow, the hock and the thigh.
