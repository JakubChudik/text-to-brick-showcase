Sample builds in 3D

Every model below was generated by a research pipeline that turns a description into a brick model you can actually build. These are the same parts and colours as the .ldr files in the repository, where each build has notes on what works and what doesn't.

Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · bricks hidden inside the model are left out to keep the files small.
