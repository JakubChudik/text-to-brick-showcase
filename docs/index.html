Seven builds in 3D

Seven models from text-to-brick, a research project that turns a description into a brick model you can build. Each one has the same parts and colours as its .ldr file in the repository, together with turntables and close-ups of its scenes.

Drag to orbit · scroll or pinch to zoom · bricks hidden inside the model are left out to keep the files small.
